event.rounds

The format

Two rounds, two different muscles. Round one tests how you talk to a machine. Round two tests what you build once it listens.

ROUND 01

Prompt Engineering Challenge

A 30-minute live challenge on a common AI platform. You'll be given a target output — your job is to craft the prompt that gets there, cleanly and precisely.

  • Creativity
  • Prompt Quality
  • Attention to Detail
  • Accuracy
ROUND 02

AI Build Challenge

Teams design a complete AI-generated output — poster, promotional video and tagline — around a brief that rewards originality as much as execution.

  • Creativity
  • AI Tool Utilization
  • Overall Presentation
🔒

Challenge brief stays sealed until the event goes live — that's part of the test. Come ready to think on your feet.
